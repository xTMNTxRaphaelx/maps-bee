A z15 quadrant is 2^28 = 268,435,456 tiles, 16384 x 16384. The two quadrants whose east edge is
exactly 180 are 16385 x 16384 = 268,451,840 — one column too wide, because
x = floor((180 + 180) / 360 * 32768) = 32768, one past the last column, 32767. The two whose west
edge is exactly -180 are one single tile over. 16,384 x 2 + 1 x 2 = 32,770, the whole excess.

The 0.005 degree offsets did their job: NW and SW are exactly 16384 columns wide, so the meridian
and equator seams are clean. Only the antimeridian edge was never offset by that rule.

Not new. The z14 defect recorded at docs/PLANET.md step 2 was "268,451,842 against 268,435,456 —
one whole tile line, 16,384, duplicated, plus 2". That plus 2 is this same west-edge tile, and it
survived the 0.005 degree fix because that fix never touched +/-180. It is in the z14 tier of the
committed atlas.

$ ./tools/pmtiles extract data/planet-z15-ne.pmtiles /tmp/probe-ne.pmtiles \
    --bbox=0.005,0.005,179.995,85.0511 --minzoom=15 --maxzoom=15 --dry-run
  Region tiles 268435456, result tile entries 52199332        (-16,384 tiles, -5,660 entries)

$ ./tools/pmtiles extract data/planet-z15-nw.pmtiles /tmp/probe-nw.pmtiles \
    --bbox=-179.995,0.005,-0.005,85.0511 --minzoom=15 --maxzoom=15 --dry-run
  Region tiles 268435456, result tile entries 43171732        (-1 tile, -1 entry)

Both land on 2^28 exactly, and both drop entries, so the surplus addressed real tiles rather than
being an artifact of the region arithmetic. 179.995 sits 54% inside the last column, which spans
179.989014 to 180.0, and -179.995 sits 46% inside column 0 — clear of a boundary the way 0.005
degrees is at the meridian.

All four entry counts are under MAX_SORTED_ENTRIES = 67,108,864. NE is the largest at 52.2M, so
the longitude split docs/PLANET.md step 2 holds in reserve for NE is not needed. The four sum to
119.4M entries, against the ~125M estimated for z15 at docs/PLANET.md step "the constraint that
shapes all of this".

Watch out: --dry-run is documented as "Calculate tiles to extract, but don't download them", but
both probes went on to log "fetching N tiles" and "Extract transferred 40 GB / 23 GB for an
archive size of 38 GB / 23 GB" against a local input. Check for and remove the output file after
a dry run rather than assuming the flag left nothing behind.
